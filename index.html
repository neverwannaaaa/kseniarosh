Where to go after class

🚶‍♂️ Dzerzhinskogo Street

pedestrian street

The city's main walking street: cozy cafés, street musicians, historic facades and the atmosphere of old Tyumen. Perfect for evening strolls and meeting new people.

Dzerzhinskogo St.

🍸 Bullwar Lounge

bar · lounge

A stylish spot with cocktails, soft sofas and pleasant music. Students and the creative crowd often gather here. Especially popular on weekends.

Gertsena St., 63

🎡 Amusement Park

entertainment

A Ferris wheel, carousels and plenty of activities for a great mood. Located in the city center next to Tsvetnoy Bulvar — a perfect place to unwind with friends.

Tsvetnoy Bulvar

🌊 Promenade

embankment

A scenic embankment along the Tura River with walking areas, observation decks and cafés. Especially beautiful at sunset. A favorite spot for photos and leisurely walks.

Tura River embankment
